Dat Thanh Nguyen

Bio x AI Researcher | Understanding nature with data | MSDS, Bioinformatics PhD

An experienced bioinformatician/biomedical data scientist with solid backgrounds in genetics, computer science, and statistics.

I hold a research fellow position based in Oslo, Norway, where I conduct research in statistical and machine learning methods for human genetics, focusing on psychiatric disorders.

Past projects involve gene regulation inference, deep learning methods for genomics, circular RNA, population-specific SNP array designs, and polygenic risk scores, where I was the lead developer of several high-performance computational methods.

Contact for collaboration: ndat(at)utexas(dot)edu
